# Portfolio and Funds APIs

Everything you need to know what you hold, what you can trade with, and whether a new
order will actually go through — positions, holdings, limits, and margin, all in one
place.

💼 [Positions](positions.md) - A consolidated, real-time view of your open and closed
positions for the day, across every supported segment — quantities bought/sold,
exposures, and settlement data.

📦 [Holdings](holdings.md) - A detailed snapshot of the securities (stocks, ETFs, etc.)
sitting in your account — current market value, average price, quantity, and sellable
quantity, for the equity portfolio you're actually holding.

💰 [Limits](limits.md) - Real-time available limits, margins, collateral, exposure, and
cash balances, filtered by segment, exchange, and product type.

🛡️ [Margins](margins.md) - Check the margin required (and your available balance) for a
new order — including Bracket Orders (BO) and Cover Orders (CO) — so you know it'll go
through *before* you place it.
